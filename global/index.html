The localization failure

A translated asset keeps the pixels and loses the mechanism.

Language is one variable. Trust cues, creator behavior, objections, category conventions and the competitive window can all change by market.

Source

Build a local reference set.

Make coverage visible: which competitors, customer language and market material inform the hypothesis.

Judge

Separate signal from stereotype.

Use qualified local judgment to challenge the brief. Treat that judgment as an input, not performance proof.

Test

Launch a bounded creative cell.

Define what changed, preserve the lineage and let the market’s agreed business KPI judge the result.

What a pilot must answer

Not “can we make local content?”

The useful question is whether a locally informed creative lineage can cross a defined business gate and teach the next iteration something reusable.

Coverage

Was the market reference set sufficient and visible enough to support the hypothesis?

Difference

Can the team state exactly which mechanism, language or scene changed and why?

Rights

Were contributor, voice, footage, likeness and market usage permissions agreed before launch?

Winner truth

Did the lineage cross the market’s agreed KPI gate under sufficient distribution?
