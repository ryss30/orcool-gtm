Qualification

Five things must be true before we call it a pilot.

Required

  • A named sponsor and approval owner.
  • A real media budget for the agreed test.
  • A business KPI and explicit winner threshold.
  • Timely, sufficiently complete delivery data.
  • Agreed scope, usage rights and commercial mechanics.

We will not frame as Performance Capital

  • An unpaid speculative pitch with no media commitment.
  • An unlimited request for free assets.
  • A test measured only on opinions or engagement.
  • A guaranteed-performance promise.
  • A public client case without explicit permission and verified evidence.

The honest status

We are validating the system, not claiming the category is solved.

Orcool has promising prior creative evidence. The Performance Capital model itself is at design-partner stage: end-to-end attribution, commercial winner mechanics and repeatability still have to be demonstrated through live, qualified pilots.

Questions

Before we discuss a test.

Does Orcool fund the media budget?

No. Orcool funds the agreed creative production scope. The advertiser funds distribution and provides the operating environment where performance can be measured.

Does “funded creative” mean unlimited free production?

No. Every pilot has a bounded scope and risk envelope agreed before production. Work outside that scope requires a new decision.

What counts as a winner?

The business gate agreed for the pilot. It may include spend and a commercial KPI, but it is never defined by creative taste alone.

What does Orcool earn?

Commercial terms are agreed per qualified pilot and tied to the agreed performance mechanics. This page does not publish a universal fee or carry formula.

Where do local contributors fit?

Local Signal and an evidence-qualified Local Jury can help select, diagnose and rewrite. They inform the work; delivery data remains winner truth.

Can we start with intelligence only?

Yes. Intelligence is the signal layer shown above and can be used independently through a compatible MCP client, without entering a Performance Capital pilot.
