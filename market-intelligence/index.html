03 / The learning loop

From local signal
to a better next decision.

01 / READ

See the market as it is.

Review local customer language, category creative and relevant context with source dates, a relevance window and gaps visible.

02 / FRAME

Form one testable hypothesis.

Connect audience tension to product proof, a creative mechanism and what your team has already tested.

03 / DECIDE

Keep the reason, not just the vote.

Your team accepts, rejects or refines the idea. A later signal can reopen the question without erasing the original reason.

04 / LEARN

Bring back the real result.

If the team runs a test, connect the execution and agreed KPI outcome to the hypothesis. An approval alone is not proof.
