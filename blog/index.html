Core definitions

The terms behind a creative decision system.

These are operational definitions, not labels for a generation product. Each one names a decision the loop must preserve.

01 · Creative decision engine

Evidence narrows the test. Paid delivery decides.

The engine connects source-visible evidence, accountable human judgment, the exact launched version and comparable market outcomes.

See the evidence-to-decision loop → 02 · Brand Lens

Approval reasons teach the decision boundary.

A fast expert loop learns the brand's yes, no and revise decisions. A slower market loop calibrates those judgments.
